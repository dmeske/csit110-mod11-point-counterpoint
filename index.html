Each round shows a debate motion and one argument from the other side. Pick the strongest rebuttal of four.   • 8 rounds, 2 from each sector: media, business, education, and government.
  • Every round uses a real Module 11 case or statistic.
  • No timer. Keyboard: press 1–4 to choose, N for the next round.
